# Module (hotel): Low season

> Addresses **empty rooms in the low season** and, indirectly, **interest and early payouts** (low-season cash flow). No global low-season occupancy benchmark is confirmed in the research file; use the property’s own history as the baseline.

## Logic

An empty room does not reduce payroll or rent: each low-season night sold contributes almost entirely to contribution margin. The goal is not to fill the low season at any cost — it is to **sell more nights without training guests to wait for discounts**.

## Levers

| Lever | Example | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Partner packages** | 2 nights + a meal at a partner restaurant + a local attraction ticket | Add value rather than cut the public rate. Check local rate-parity rules; in the EEA, Booking.com cannot prevent properties from offering different prices on their own websites under the DMA. [Research](../../mercados/global-pesquisa-2026-10.md) |
| **Regional market** | A “weekend without a public holiday” offer for nearby cities and drive markets | Use consented email or SMS lists and regional advertising. Follow local messaging and privacy rules, including TCPA, PECR, GDPR/UK GDPR, or CASL where applicable. [Research](../../mercados/global-pesquisa-2026-10.md) |
| **Corporate and events** | Conferences, training sessions, and midweek weddings | Use the city’s events calendar; negotiate rates that are not publicly listed |
| **Minimum stay during peaks** | Set a minimum stay around major local events or peak travel dates | Can shift arrival and departure nights beyond the peak; confirm demand and local booking terms |
| **Demand-based pricing** | Set rates by weekday and booking lead time | Do not assume rate parity applies everywhere. Check local rules and the property’s OTA agreements. [Research](../../mercados/global-pesquisa-2026-10.md) |
| **Guest membership or club** | “3 low-season nights per year,” paid by card or a locally available instant payment method | Creates recurring revenue for quieter periods. Confirm whether a suitable recurring-payment method is available locally; FedNow, Faster Payments/Pay by Bank, and SEPA Instant are not interchangeable with card subscriptions. [Research](../../mercados/global-pesquisa-2026-10.md) |
| **Local community** | Build a network with restaurants and attractions (a city guide) | See [local marketing](marketing-local.md) |

## Low-season cash flow

Deposits on peak-season bookings plus a 12-week cash forecast can reduce reliance on early card payouts and overdrafts in the low season. Card fees and instant-payment options vary by market; no single global merchant fee is confirmed in the research. See [billing](cobranca.md) and [research](../../mercados/global-pesquisa-2026-10.md).

## Checklist

- [ ] Monthly occupancy and average daily rate for the previous year (baseline)
- [ ] City and regional events calendar for the next 12 months
- [ ] 3 partner packages agreed (added value, no public discount)
- [ ] Minimum-stay rule for peak dates
- [ ] Regional campaign for consented contacts ahead of each weak month
- [ ] 12-week cash forecast

## How to measure

Compare occupancy and average daily rate for each low-season month with the same month in the previous year; track package nights sold, partner revenue, and financing costs during the low season.
