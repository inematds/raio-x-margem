# Module (hotel): Direct bookings without breaking rate parity

> Addresses: **OTAs charging guests who already know your property**, **visibility programs that do not pay for themselves**, **guests who do not return**, **ancillary revenue**, **manual quotes**, **no-shows**. Contract rules: [hotel sector guide](../../setores/hotel.md#contracts--what-changes-in-the-strategy).

## The rule that governs everything

Rate parity rules depend on the market and the accommodation agreement. In the EEA, Booking.com cannot prevent accommodation providers from offering different prices on their own websites under the DMA, effective 14 November 2024. In the UK and Australia, the research does not confirm a general ban on narrow parity clauses. Check the contract and local rules in other markets before changing public rates. [Research](../../mercados/global-pesquisa-2026-10.md)

The direct channel can win through:

1. **Private rates** — shared directly by email, SMS, at the front desk, or with a consented member list. Check the agreement and local rules before offering rates that are not publicly listed.
2. **Benefits instead of discounts** — breakfast included, late checkout, an upgrade subject to availability, a bottle of wine, or credit at a partner restaurant.
3. **Convenience** — a fast booking engine, card payment or a supported local instant payment option, immediate confirmation, and direct contact with the property.

## Building blocks

| Block | Purpose | Path A (ready-made) | Path B (open) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Booking engine | book and pay on the website | the property’s PMS or booking engine; compare available providers locally | for a small property: calendar + card processing or a supported local payment method + manual confirmation |
| Consented guest list | contact people who have already stayed | check-in form with opt-in (paper or digital) | spreadsheet/CRM ([open alternatives](../ALTERNATIVAS-ABERTAS.md)) |
| Pre-arrival check-in | sell extras and collect opt-in | message 3 days before arrival with a link | custom form |
| Guarantee | reduce no-shows | policy + deposit using a locally supported payment method | card payment or local instant payment; verify availability and terms locally ([payments](cobranca.md)) |
| Preferred program test | see whether the added commission pays off | compare 2 months with and without the program, against the same period last year | channel spreadsheet |

## What **not** to do

- Publish a lower price on your site if your agreement or local rules prohibit it; check the rules for your market.
- Message guests using contact details supplied by an OTA unless the agreement and applicable consent rules allow it.
- Invite guests to “book direct next time” inside an OTA’s or Airbnb’s messaging system if the platform rules prohibit it.
- Advertise using an OTA’s brand without permission.

## Guest journey (with consent collected by the property)

1. Check-in: “Would you like to receive our low-season offers and guest rates?” (record the opt-in).
2. During the stay: place a card in the room with a QR code for the guest club.
3. Check-out + 2 days: send a thank-you and request a Google review.
4. Stay anniversary or low season: send a private guest rate by a permitted channel.

Use SMS or email where appropriate, and follow the applicable consent rules, such as TCPA in the US, PECR in the UK, GDPR in the EU/UK, and CASL in Canada. Verify local rules in other markets. [Research](../../mercados/global-pesquisa-2026-10.md)

## Checklist

- [ ] Revenue and commission by channel for the last 12 months (to account for seasonality)
- [ ] Website booking engine with card payment and any supported local instant payment option, tested on a phone
- [ ] Private rates and benefits defined in writing, with public-rate and contract rules checked
- [ ] Check-in opt-in + spreadsheet/CRM
- [ ] Pre-arrival message with 3 extras
- [ ] Guarantee policy published and applied
- [ ] Preferred/visibility program test scheduled (with a review date)

## How to measure

Direct-channel revenue as a share of total revenue versus OTA revenue, month by month against the **same month last year**; total commission paid; share of bookings with an extra; uncollected no-show charges; and 12-month return rate by guest cohort.
